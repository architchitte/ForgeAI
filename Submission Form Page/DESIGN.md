## Brand & Style

This design system channels the terrifying precision and stark beauty of general relativity: the boundary layer where light bends, space collapses, and raw energy radiates from the accretion disk against absolute zero void. 

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Absolute Precision & Cold Intellect:** Utilitarian, highly scientific, zero fluff. The interface evokes mission-critical telemetry, gravitational wave detectors, and deep-space terminal instrumentation.
- **Controlled Cosmic Energy:** The terrifying stillness of pitch-black vacuum punctuated by violent, coherent photons—blazing plasma orange and glowing accretion embers.
- **Cognitive Clarity:** Dense metrics, complex graphs, and deep operational interfaces remain effortlessly legible against pitch-black backgrounds through deliberate typographic contrast and subtle optical glows.

### Aesthetics & Stylistic Direction
The style merges **Futuristic Technical Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Radial Depth**:
- **Void Foundations:** Backgrounds are true void black, preventing muddy gray casts and preserving absolute dynamic range.
- **Accretion Photonics:** Light is used sparingly as high-voltage functional indicators: linear gradients along razor-sharp borders mimicking the photon sphere, and localized radial auras around critical actions.
- **Gravitational Wireframes:** Subtle translucent structural grids and low-opacity obsidian borders anchor layouts, evoking astronomical coordinate systems and relativistic lensing grids.

## Colors

The palette derives strictly from the thermodynamics of black holes: the total darkness of the interior singularity bounded by the intense, radiant heat of the photon ring.

### Functional Palette System
- **Void Obsidian (`#030407`):** The singularity bedrock. Used for app canvases, base viewports, and deep-layer terminals.
- **Deep Navy Obsidian (`#06080D` to `#0B0F19`):** Gravitational mantle. Used for elevated cards, nested panels, sidebars, and contextual dialogs.
- **Stellar Dust Borders (`#141B2D` to `#1E293B`):** Ghost outlines, coordinate grid divisions, and hairline container separators.
- **Photon Ring Plasma (`#FF6B1A`):** Primary functional active state. Focus rings, key action buttons, telemetry alerts, and live operational nodes.
- **Radiant Amber (`#FFA336`):** Secondary data visualization, warning markers, hovering states, and radiant photon gradients.
- **Relativistic Singularity Core (`#FF4500`):** Critical alerts, threshold warnings, active laser triggers, and high-energy telemetry surges.
- **Cosmic Ash Neutrals (`#94A3B8` to `#F8FAFC`):** Crisp data typography scaling from distant starlight (muted slate) to super-bright pulsar white.

### Application Rules
1. Never substitute soft gray for void bases. Dark mode is permanent, stark, and absolute.
2. The orange-amber-plasma spectrum must strictly signify **active energy, real-time data flow, focus, or system state**. Do not use primary colors for large passive decorative planes.
3. Ambient halos must use ultra-low opacity tints (`rgba(255, 107, 26, 0.08)` to `0.15`) feathered outward to suggest gravitational light bending without muddying contrast.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is structured like an astrophysical flight deck: mathematical clarity balanced against cutting-edge technical architecture.

### Typographic Pairing Logic
- **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers clean geometry, subtle futuristic tension, and sharp technical attitude without devolving into unreadable novelty.
- **Reading & General UI (`Geist`):** Crisp, neutral, developer-centric sans with supreme micro-legibility at 12–16px, ideal for high-density information architecture.
- **Metrics, Telemetry & Tags (`JetBrains Mono`):** Fixed-width baseline ensuring zero layout shift during continuous numerical stream updates, paired with an industrial all-caps sub-labeling discipline.

### Style Directives
- Apply `text-transform: uppercase` paired with `letterSpacing: 0.08em` for status badges, section headers, column labels, and terminal callouts using `label-caps`.
- Code blocks, coordinate pairs, SHA hashes, and memory counters must strictly render in `JetBrains Mono` with zero ligatures to prevent glyph misinterpretation.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts in this design system follow an uncompromising 12-column coordinate matrix built for dense technical workflows, real-time command centers, and analytical consoles.

### Grid & Density Hierarchy
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column layout with 20px (`1.25rem`) gutters and 32px (`2rem`) outer margins. Content spreads laterally into multi-pane docked workspaces, telemetry ribbons, and persistent inspector trays.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters. Panels dock into collapsible side-drawers; analytical visualizations pivot to stacked views.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters and 16px (`1rem`) margins. Multi-column metric displays condense into modular swipeable cards or single-column linear stacks.

### Spacing Principles
- Component layout uses strict multiples of 4px/8px. 
- Tight padding (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) is favored inside dense data modules, code tables, and parameter pills to maintain maximum field of view.
- Generous outer structural gaps (`space-lg`, `space-xl`) separate entire functional horizons, creating void-like breaks between distinct sub-systems.

## Elevation & Depth

In a black hole environment, physical shadows are meaningless; depth is expressed through **event horizon tiering, relativistic photon bleeding, and crystalline opacity**.

### Depth Layers
1. **The Singularity Canvas (Base):** `#030407` pitch black. Completely non-reflective. Background coordinate lines etched at 4% white opacity.
2. **Deep Navy Obsidian (Container Tier 1):** `#06080D` with a subtle hairline border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`. Creates structural separation without drop shadows.
3. **Photon Perimeter (Container Tier 2 / Interactive):** `#0B0F19` backdrop with an interior top hairline gradient border transition (`rgba(255, 107, 26, 0.4)` to `rgba(255, 163, 54, 0.0)`).
4. **Accretion Flare (Overlays & Dialogs):** `#080C14` at 92% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and an ambient photon aura: `box-shadow: 0 0 32px -8px rgba(255, 107, 26, 0.22)`.

### Border-First Elevation
Drop shadows are prohibited for standard UI cards. Depth is established purely by:
- Micro-borders: 1px continuous borders tinted from subtle cosmic dust (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.6)`) to hot plasma glow (`rgba(255, 107, 26, 0.5)`).
- Edge highlights: 1px semi-transparent white or amber linear gradient highlights along the top edge of cards simulating radiant overhead starlight.

## Shapes

The geometry reflects aerodynamic spacecraft bulkheads and astrophysical instrumentation: disciplined, low-radius curvature that maintains engineering rigor.

### Shape Characteristics
- **Level 1 (Soft / Micro-radius):** Base interactive controls, metric tags, terminal windows, and code cells use an exacting 4px (`0.25rem`) corner radius.
- **Compound Cards & Panels (`rounded-lg`):** Scaled to 8px (`0.5rem`) to create smooth yet assertive physical envelopes for data containers and modals.
- **Accretion Curve Overrides:** Specific peripheral indicators, laser trackers, and status tags may adopt asymmetrical chamfers or micro-pill radiuses (9999px) strictly when denoting orbital paths or binary sensor statuses.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Photon Blast):** Solid radiant gradient (`#FF6B1A` to `#FF4500`). Text is solid `#030407` (`Space Grotesk` Medium). Hover introduces a subtle, blazing plasma outer aura: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(255, 107, 26, 0.45)`.
- **Secondary (Obsidian Shell):** Background `#0B0F19`, 1px border `rgba(255, 107, 26, 0.3)`. Text is `#FFA336`. Hover shifts the border to 100% `#FF6B1A` with a background flood of `rgba(255, 107, 26, 0.08)`.
- **Ghost (Terminal Link):** No background, borderless. Text is `#94A3B8`. Hover transitions text to `#F8FAFC` accompanied by an orange-bracket indicator prefix `[ ]`.

### Chips & Telemetry Badges
- Constructed with `label-caps` in `JetBrains Mono`.
- Background `rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.8)` with a 1px border.
- Features a pulsating 6px circular dot indicator: blazing green (`#00FF88`) for active nodes, radiant plasma (`#FF6B1A`) for gravitational surge, and dark carbon (`#475569`) for offline relays.

### Input Fields & Terminal Controls
- Background `#06080D`, border 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Placeholder text in muted dust `#475569`.
- Typography is strictly `Geist` or `JetBrains Mono`.
- **Focus State:** 1px solid border `#FF6B1A` accompanied by an interior hairline glow `box-shadow: inset 0 0 6px rgba(255, 107, 26, 0.2)`. No thick default browser outlines.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Checkbox: 16x16px square with 3px border-radius, deep obsidian fill, 1px border `#334155`. Checked state fills `#FF6B1A` with an SVG sharp checkmark in pitch black `#030407`.
- Radio: Concentric dual-ring design. Active state triggers an inner amber `#FFA336` core radiating into an orange perimeter ring.

### Cards & Telemetry Pods
- Background `#06080D` with top-border highlight gradient: `linear-gradient(90deg, rgba(255,107,26,0.3) 0%, rgba(255,163,54,0.05) 50%, rgba(255,255,255,0) 100%)`.
- Hairline structural border in `rgba(20, 27, 45, 0.9)`.
- Header houses `Space Grotesk` titles paired with trailing monospace status counters.

### Lists & Coordinate Tables
- Strict zebra-less layout. Rows are separated by 1px hairline rules in `#0F172A`.
- Hovering over a row triggers a low-intensity left-border notch (2px `#FF6B1A`) and a delicate row back-fill of `rgba(255, 107, 26, 0.03)`.
- Monospace numerical values align to tabular-nums for vertical alignment.

### Bespoke System Components
- **Singularity Data Horizon (HUD Progress Bar):** Ultra-thin 2px track in `#141B2D` containing a liquid plasma `#FF6B1A` gradient fill with a blazing leading-edge blur particle mimicking an orbiting photon.
- **Relativistic Code Block:** Framed inside `#04060A`, numbered via muted starlight indexes, with syntax highlighting emphasizing plasma oranges, hot magentas, and deep cyans.
